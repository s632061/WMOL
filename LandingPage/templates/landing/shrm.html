A Closer Look at the System

The Product

A structured path to the SHRM-CP certification.

WMOL HR Certification Prep brings the learning, performance, and emotional stability systems together to help you reach the certification without spending time and effort on the things that don’t matter.

It puts the study resources, practice questions, progression, and next steps in one place so you can focus on reaching the goal instead of figuring out the entire preparation process yourself.

The software brings those systems together across platforms so you can use the systems to reach your goals at your convenience.

What Does It Do?

Preparing for the SHRM exam is already hard enough.

You shouldn’t also have to:

  • Figure out which resources to use.
  • Separate what you need to know from what is merely nice to know.
  • Translate explanations that are harder than they need to be.
  • Figure out how HR concepts connect.
  • Learn how the exam turns those concepts into questions.
  • Decide what to study next.
  • Decide what to practice next.
  • Keep reviewing material you already understand.
  • Guess whether you’re ready for the exam.

The WMOL learning system organizes or removes as much of that surrounding work as possible so more of your time and effort can go toward becoming capable of passing the exam.

This way you can focus on understanding HR and how to apply it to decisions about people, policy, and the workplace, without losing time organizing the preparation itself.

How It Works

We work out the path first. Then we turn it into a system you can follow.

We take the goal, work through the material and exam ourselves, and identify what actually helps you prepare. We turn that work into a step-by-step path connecting the study resources, practice questions, corrections, and review so you only focus on what actually matters.

The learning system shows you how to use those pieces to understand, remember, and apply what matters.

The performance system organizes how much work to do, how to progress, and when to recover so you can keep moving toward the certification with time and energy for the rest of your life.

The emotional stability system helps you keep learning, apply what you know, and trust your preparation even when you have a lot going on.

Where It’s Been Used

Built and tested in some of the most demanding environments.

The learning system was created and stress-tested through medical education and military academic training, including preparation that led to early passes on the first two US medical licensing exams.

The performance system was created and stress-tested through strength and ultra-endurance training, including a 600-pound deadlift, preparation for 100-mile races, and a 13.1-mile open-water swim.

The emotional stability system was developed to help people keep learning, applying what they know, and trusting their preparation through periods of intense personal and professional pressure.

We’ve brought all three systems together for SHRM preparation so you can learn what matters, manage the work, and keep moving toward the certification when life gets demanding.

Why It Works for You

Everyone may start in different places, but we all use the same tools to reach our goals.

Whatever our background or starting point, moving toward a difficult goal still requires:

  • Learning.
  • Practicing.
  • Adapting.
  • Recovering.
  • Continuing forward.

All the WMOL systems do is organize those pieces around the:

  • Right time.

So you can keep working toward the certification while keeping up with the people and things that matter to you.
