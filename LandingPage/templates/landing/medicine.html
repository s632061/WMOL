The Learning System

A Four-Part Repeatable Learning System

Students are taught a four-part system that teaches them how to use all of the resources provided by your institution in the most efficient manner.

Understand

Students figure out what matters, what they need to know, and how to use the resources around them to actually understand it.

Remember

Students turn that understanding into something they can retrieve and use later instead of repeatedly starting over.

Apply

Students use practice questions to test what they actually know and find the parts that still need work.

Stay Consistent

Students learn how to keep the system working as the amount of information, pressure, and responsibility increases.

The Four-Week Program

How the Four-Week Program Works

Each week teaches a different part of the system, then gives students a chance to use it before the full pressure of medical school begins.

Week 1

Understand and Build the Foundation

Learners learn the workflow, select resources, identify essential information, build conceptual understanding, and establish a manageable daily rhythm.

Week 2

Apply and Reveal Gaps

Learners begin consistent question-based application to determine whether understanding can be retrieved and used rather than merely recognized.

Week 3

Correct and Consolidate

Learners revisit missed questions, identify where their reasoning broke down, and refine incomplete working memories until weak areas become more reliable.

Week 4

Stabilize Under Simulated Conditions

Learners complete randomized sets combining prior and new material in order to build confidence and competence in simulated exam environments.
