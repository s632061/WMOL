Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Wang Method LLC (“Wang Method,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information in connection with the CompTIA Security+ Companion App (the “App”). Learning data stays on your device while cloud progress sync is unavailable or you are signed out. Cloud progress sync is not currently enabled in the Security+ mobile app. In a version that enables it, signing into WMOL automatically synchronizes eligible study progress with that account while the app is open and connected. Information you choose to send to support is handled separately. Where Connect your devices is available, an optional WMOL account connects eligible subscription access across devices. This Policy describes that sign-in, billing, and access-verification data as well as local storage.

1) Key points

We do not sell personal information.

We do not use learning progress for third-party advertising or cross-app tracking.

The current App does not include a third-party advertising SDK.

Most detailed learning and practice data remains on your device.

Premium verification and encrypted content delivery require limited network requests to service providers.

On supported mobile versions, local reminders are optional and controlled through device permissions.

Optional connected access uses authentication and device records. Account sign-in alone does not enable an unavailable progress-sync feature. In versions with cloud progress sync enabled, eligible learning history is stored with your authenticated WMOL account automatically after sign-in.

We do not use precise location or geographic restrictions to enforce the connected-device limit.

2) Information stored on your device

Depending on the features you use, the App may store:

A name or nickname used for in-App personalization

Theme, reminder, onboarding, walkthrough, and other settings

Study-plan progress and completion records

Practice attempts, answers, outcomes, omitted items, review groups, paused sessions, queues, history, and resume data

Downloaded-content records, file paths, versions, verification hashes, and encrypted content packs

Notification permission state and scheduled local-notification identifiers

Premium entitlement state, a pseudonymous RevenueCat App User ID / Support ID, and time-limited offline authorization

When using connected access, sign-in session tokens, the displayed sign-in label, a device identifier, and private device credentials; mobile and desktop cache encryption keys and private device credentials use operating-system secure storage

Popup, greeting, and milestone-completion state

This information is used to operate the App, preserve your place, display progress, resume sessions, and manage downloaded content. It generally remains on your device unless a feature described below requires a network request or you choose to send information to us.

3) Learning and practice data

Cloud progress sync is not currently enabled in the Security+ mobile app. In a version that enables it, progress sync starts automatically when you sign into WMOL; there is no separate sync switch. This section describes the data that such a version would process. Wang Method LLC processes your study completion and resource identifiers, saved answers, correct/wrong/omitted outcomes, practice attempt history and timestamps, paused session order and position, review and randomization state, study bookmarks, guide completion and qualifying exam-readiness records. Practice history may include the associated question text needed to display past attempts. These records are linked to your authenticated WMOL account identifier, rather than matched by email text, and stored through our existing Cloudflare Workers and D1 service. Security+, AWS and SHRM records are kept separate.

We use this information to preserve your place, combine work from your devices, show your learning history, resolve conflicting edits and restore eligible progress. We do not use it for third-party advertising, cross-app tracking or referral-partner reporting. Your local nickname, notification permissions, scheduled reminders, theme, store credentials, sign-in tokens, purchase receipts and content decryption keys are not part of the study-progress upload. Authentication, billing and technical-request data are handled separately as described below.

While signed out, study data stays on the device except information you choose to send to support. In versions with cloud progress sync enabled, signing in assigns unassigned local study work to that account and synchronizes its progress automatically. Local copies support offline use. Signing out stops synchronization for that account on this device without deleting existing cloud records. Local recovery copies may be kept when edits conflict or a cloud reset replaces old offline work; they are not automatically uploaded.

You can use the App’s reset controls for supported progress data. You can also clear App storage or delete the App through your device. Deleting the App or a WMOL account does not cancel a subscription.

4) Optional sign-in, connected devices, and subscription identifiers

The App uses RevenueCat to support purchases, restore access, and determine whether Premium is active. A purchase may use a random device App User ID or an identifier associated with a connected WMOL account. The Support ID is a troubleshooting reference, not proof of ownership or a sign-in code. We also retain hashed store transaction references and opaque purchase-connection identifiers to restore eligible purchases and prevent one purchase from being claimed by different WMOL accounts. These ownership records do not contain full payment-card details and may remain after account deletion.

Where available, Continue with Apple or Google authenticates you through the chosen provider and Supabase. Depending on the provider and your choices, authentication may process your provider identifier, email address or Apple relay address, basic profile information such as a name when supplied by the provider, and authentication/session records. The App displays a sign-in label so you can recognize the connected account. We do not receive your Apple or Google password.

The Cloudflare-hosted access service stores the WMOL user identifier, its RevenueCat mapping, device identifiers and labels, hashed private device credentials, connection status and timestamps, and device-replacement timing. These records connect an eligible purchase to one account, enforce the two-device limit and replacement cooldown, and verify access. Short-lived connection requests and browser confirmation codes support sign-in; codes and Support IDs do not independently authorize Premium.

RevenueCat may process App User IDs, products, offerings, purchase events, subscription and entitlement status, expiration information, purchase environment, and related technical information. The App and access service use these records and authentication/device proof to authorize protected content and issue bounded offline access. These identifiers and purchase records also support RevenueCat subscription analytics and customer history. Study progress uploads only in versions with cloud progress sync enabled, while you are signed in and the app is open and connected. There is no separate progress-sync switch.

5) Purchases and billing email

Apple or Google processes marketplace purchases. Where web billing is offered, RevenueCat Billing and Stripe process checkout, payment, billing email, receipts, and subscription management. Wang Method LLC does not receive your full payment-card number. The relevant providers may process transaction, tax, fraud-prevention, account, device, and subscription information under their own privacy practices and legal obligations. We may access billing/customer and transaction records made available through our billing services for support, reconciliation, refunds, and abuse prevention.

Entering an email at web checkout does not automatically connect a purchase to a WMOL sign-in. Billing and sign-in records serve different purposes. Signing in or refreshing access does not create a new charge; an existing subscription may still renew on its normal schedule.

5a) Optional referrals and purchase-event records

Where referral tracking is enabled for a supported product and purchase channel, our Cloudflare-hosted service receives and stores selected purchase and subscription events from RevenueCat, including events for purchases without a referral code. These records can include pseudonymous customer, account, device, and alias identifiers; transaction and original-subscription identifiers; product and store; test or production environment; event and purchase times; subscription changes; and payment amounts, currency, and available tax or store-fee information.

If you choose to enter a referral code, we validate it and associate an accepted code with the verified original subscription, the submitting customer or account identifier, and the time of the claim. We use these records to verify eligibility, avoid duplicate or reassigned claims, attribute eligible purchases and renewals, review refunds or cancellations, and reconcile referral payments. The current referral event record excludes email addresses, full payment-card details, raw store receipts, and sign-in tokens. Pseudonymous identifiers and transaction references are still personal information, not anonymous data.

The partner-summary report contains aggregate transaction counts and payment totals by partner, environment, currency, and status; it does not contain buyer, email, device, or receipt identifiers. Detailed transaction and attribution reports are retained for internal administration and support. Entering a code does not share your study history with a referral partner.

Deleting a WMOL account does not automatically erase referral claims, purchase events, or related attribution and reconciliation records. The current referral database has no automatic expiry for those records. You may request access, correction, deletion, or review through WangMethodOfLearning@gmail.com; requests are considered under applicable rights and permitted accounting, security, fraud-prevention, and legal retention.

6) Encrypted content delivery and Cloudflare

The App connects to Wang Method LLC infrastructure hosted through Cloudflare to retrieve a content manifest, weekly plans, encrypted content packs, and authorized decryption keys.

These requests may involve:

Requested pack ID and key ID

A pseudonymous RevenueCat App User ID when Premium verification is required

Authentication and private device proof for supported connected-access and device-verification requests

IP address, request time, network information, user-agent or device-request metadata, security signals, and standard server-log information processed by hosting and security providers

This information is used to deliver content, verify authorization, maintain service security, prevent abuse, diagnose failures, and protect infrastructure. Downloaded proprietary packs are stored locally in encrypted form and are decrypted for authorized use. No security method can guarantee absolute security.

7) Local notifications

On mobile versions that support reminders, if you enable them, the App requests notification permission and schedules notifications locally on your device. Reminder text may use the name or nickname stored locally. Core learning content does not require notification permission, and you can disable notifications through device settings.

8) Feedback, support, email, and clipboard

If you contact us or send feedback by email, Wang Method LLC receives the information you choose to provide, which may include your email address, message, screenshots, device platform, App details, and Support ID. We use that information to respond, troubleshoot, prevent abuse, improve the App, and maintain reasonable support records.

The App can copy your Support ID to the clipboard when you ask it to. The App is not designed to read unrelated clipboard content. Do not send confidential employer information, official exam content, trade secrets, or sensitive personal information in feedback or support messages.

11) Retention

Local App information remains on your device until you use applicable reset controls, clear App storage, or delete the App, subject to operating-system behavior.

Support emails and records controlled by Wang Method LLC may be retained as reasonably necessary to respond, document the issue, maintain security, enforce agreements, resolve disputes, or comply with legal obligations, and may later be deleted or de-identified when appropriate.

Optional account and connection records are kept while needed to provide and secure connected access. Deleting a WMOL account removes its Supabase authentication user and its active account/device associations in the access database. The current access service retains a pseudonymous purchase/device claim record, including device and RevenueCat identifiers, a hashed device credential, and a previously-claimed marker, to prevent the same purchase being repeatedly assigned to new accounts. It removes the former WMOL user association from that device record. This retained record is not anonymous and does not automatically expire in the current implementation. You may request review of retained personal information at the contact address below, subject to applicable rights and permitted security, fraud-prevention, or legal retention.

The access service also retains purchase-ownership and transaction-mapping records, including hashed transaction references, pseudonymous original-purchaser and connected-account identifiers, and an original-device identifier where recorded. These records support verified restores and prevent a deleted account from being used to reassign a purchase. Account deletion does not erase those ownership records or automatically release the purchase to a new account. They have no automatic expiry in the current implementation. You may request review or deletion through the contact below, subject to applicable rights and permitted retention.

Referral-record retention is described in section 5a. These records are separate from the sign-in and active device connections removed by Delete Account.

Where a version has enabled cloud progress sync, active cloud study records are retained while the account exists, subject to study records you remove through the app and account deletion. Earlier releases may have offered a cloud-profile reset. Existing reset versions continue to prevent older offline changes from being uploaded automatically. Individual removal markers may remain while the account exists to reconcile older devices; they do not retain the removed answer or attempt contents.

Delete Account removes the cloud study records, blocks further uploads for that deleted account, and removes its study profile and recovery copies on the device performing deletion. A minimal account identifier and deletion marker are retained to reject stale requests; this marker contains no study answers or practice history and currently has no automatic expiry. Offline copies on other devices may remain until their app data is cleared. They cannot upload into the deleted account. Provider backup and security records may remain under the provider’s retention process and are not used to restore a deleted study profile. Other retained purchase, referral, transaction and support records are described separately in this Policy.

Billing, transaction, security-log, backup, and support records may remain after in-App deletion where needed for their stated purpose or applicable obligations. RevenueCat, Stripe, Supabase, Apple, Google, Cloudflare, email providers, and other service providers also process and retain information under their respective roles, policies, and legal obligations. In-App deletion does not automatically erase independent store or payment-provider records.

12) Deletion, access, correction, and export requests

Where optional sign-in is available, open Settings > Full Access > Connect your devices > Delete Account to delete your WMOL account. You need to have signed in recently and confirm the deletion. This removes your WMOL sign-in and disconnects your devices, while the limited records described above are kept. It does not delete your Apple or Google account, cancel billing, or erase offline copies on other devices. Cloud study records and the account’s study profile on the device performing deletion are removed. Use Manage Subscription before deleting if you want to stop future charges. Creating another WMOL account, even with the same email, will not restore this purchase automatically. Contact support if you need help with that purchase.

You may also request account deletion without reinstalling the App, or request access to, correction of, deletion of, or a copy of personal information reasonably controlled by Wang Method LLC, by emailing WangMethodOfLearning@gmail.com and identifying the CompTIA Security+ App and your request. We may need reasonable information to verify ownership. Never send passwords, sign-in codes, or private device credentials. We will address verified requests and relevant service-provider data as required by applicable law.

We cannot remotely retrieve, export, or delete information that exists only on your device. We also do not control marketplace, payment, tax, fraud-prevention, or independent service-provider records. Requests concerning those records may need to be directed to the relevant provider.

13) Security

We use technical and organizational measures appropriate to the App’s current architecture, including encrypted content packs, integrity verification, entitlement checks for protected keys, device-protected key storage where supported, and limited network authorization requests. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure.
